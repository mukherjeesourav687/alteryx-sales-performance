My first steps with Alteryx started with a simple task: combine five monthly CSV files and summarise product quantities.

I have now expanded that practice project into a sales-performance workflow with:

• Numeric and date conversions, plus text cleaning
• Validation rules and duplicate-order checks
• Sales-value calculations and percentage contribution
• Product, monthly and regional summaries
• Separate outputs for records that need review
• A clearly labelled canvas to make the process easier to follow

The full workflow ran successfully on 50 practice records, covering 253 units.

One finding stood out: Mouse contributed 40.3% of units sold, while Laptop contributed 63.7% of sales value. Comparing volume and value gave me a much clearer view of product performance.

I am still early in my Alteryx learning journey. This project helped me understand how data preparation, quality checks and analysis fit together in a repeatable workflow.
